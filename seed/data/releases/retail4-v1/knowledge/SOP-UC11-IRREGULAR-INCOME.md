# Side-hustle income and planning (SOP-UC11-IRREGULAR-INCOME)

Synthetic Contoso demo procedure - fictional internal policy, not legal or regulatory authority.

**Applies to:** UC11   **Scope:** case   **Source version:** 1

## Purpose
This procedure guides bank staff on discussing planning where income is irregular from freelance or side-hustle work. Staff must anchor the discussion to money actually received, keep unpaid work separate from available funds, and avoid drawing conclusions from social visibility or informal collaboration signals.

## 1. Scope and intent

Use this procedure when a customer is planning around irregular freelance, project-based or side-hustle income.
Keep the discussion practical, factual and limited to what the customer can presently rely upon.

- Apply the procedure consistently for case UC11.
- Treat this as internal Contoso demo policy material for a fictional demonstration setting.

## 2. Core review steps

Start by identifying funds that have actually come in and distinguish them from expected receipts. Where project terms changed after initial agreement, clarify the original scope and the later requests before discussing timing or amount expectations.

- Identify which amounts are invoiced but unpaid; treat receivables as not being cash.

## 3. Assumptions staff must not make

Do not convert uncertain or informal signals into factual financial capacity. If a point has not been evidenced in the conversation or records provided for the discussion, leave it as uncertain.

- Do not treat a collaboration message as a verified brand partnership.
- Do not draw conclusions from social presence, audience size or engagement metrics.

## 4. When to pause the discussion

If expected payment timing cannot be established even approximately, the planning discussion cannot be made reliable. In such cases, pause and keep the discussion only at a general level until the customer can provide a reasonable estimate.

## 5. Permitted planning outcomes

Any planning discussed under this procedure must remain conservative and workable with irregular income. Do not frame any option as preferred or suitable unless it fits the customer's actual received cash flow pattern.

## Required checks
- Confirm which client payments have actually been received.
- Identify which amounts are invoiced but unpaid; a receivable is not cash.
- Check the agreed scope against what was later requested.

## Prohibited assumptions
- Do not assume that an invoice will be paid, or when it will be paid.
- Do not infer income, creditworthiness or business identity from social reach.
- Do not assume a verified brand partnership from a collaboration message.

## Stop conditions
- Stop if payment timing is unknown and the customer cannot estimate it.

## Valid outcomes
- Plan on received income only.
- Recommend nothing that requires a fixed monthly commitment he cannot meet.

## Human gate
Only a human staff member may authorise any case-specific guidance that relies on judgement about uncertain payment timing or disputed scope.
