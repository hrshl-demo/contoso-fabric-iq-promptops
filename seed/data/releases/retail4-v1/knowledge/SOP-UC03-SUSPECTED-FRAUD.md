# Suspicious contact handling (SOP-UC03-SUSPECTED-FRAUD)

Synthetic Contoso demo procedure - fictional internal policy, not legal or regulatory authority.

**Applies to:** UC03   **Scope:** case   **Source version:** 1

## Purpose
This procedure sets out how bank staff must respond when a customer reports a message claiming to be from the bank. The aim is to verify the situation carefully, explain any risk indicators clearly, and keep the interaction focused only on customer safety.

## 1. Scope and principle

This is a Synthetic Contoso demo procedure - fictional internal policy, not legal or regulatory authority.
Use this procedure for customer contacts about calls, SMS, email, chat messages or similar communications that claim to be from the bank.
Treat the matter as a safety interaction. Do not turn the conversation into any sales or product discussion.

- Keep the discussion limited to verifying the reported contact and explaining safety concerns.
- Use clear, calm language and avoid creating certainty where the facts are not yet established.

## 2. Initial handling

Obtain the customer's description of the message, who contacted them, what was asked, and whether the customer acted on it.
Clarify the exact callback number or contact details shown in the message and what the customer was asked to do.

## 3. Assessment rules

Do not assume a message is fraudulent only because it mentions the bank. Assess the reported facts and the warning signs present.
Do not treat branding, logos, formatting or a reference number as proof that a message is authentic.
Where warning signs are present, explain the exact signals that are concerning and why they increase risk.

- If the message seeks a code, a mandate approval or any credential, treat that as a significant warning sign requiring careful handling.
- If the callback number does not match the number printed on the customer's card, treat that as a concerning discrepancy.

## 4. Escalation and stop rule

If there is any indication that the customer may already have approved something, stop routine handling and escalate immediately.
Do not continue with normal reassurance or extended discussion once the stop condition is met; move the case forward without delay.

- Escalate immediately if the customer may already have approved something.

## 5. Customer communication outcome

Where appropriate, explain which specific signals are concerning and why, using only the facts reported and verified during the interaction.
If the notice is verified as genuine, confirm that the genuine notice is genuine.
No product is discussed on a safety matter under any circumstances.

- Selling is prohibited in this interaction.
- Do not introduce, describe or promote any product while handling the safety matter.

## Required checks
- Check whether the callback number matches the number printed on the customer's card.
- Check whether the message asks for a code, a mandate approval or a credential.
- Check whether any money or mandate has actually been authorised.

## Prohibited assumptions
- Do not assume that every message mentioning the bank is fraudulent.
- Do not assume that branding or a reference number establishes authenticity.

## Stop conditions
- Stop routine handling and escalate immediately if the customer may already have approved something.

## Valid outcomes
- Explain which specific signals are concerning and why.
- Confirm a genuine notice is genuine.
- No product is discussed on a safety matter under any circumstances.

## Human gate
Only a human staff member may authorise the final confirmation that a reported notice is genuine or decide the final escalation action.
