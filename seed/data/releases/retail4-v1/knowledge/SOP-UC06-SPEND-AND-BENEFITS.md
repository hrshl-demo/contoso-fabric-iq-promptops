# Spending review and card suitability (SOP-UC06-SPEND-AND-BENEFITS)

Synthetic Contoso demo procedure - fictional internal policy, not legal or regulatory authority.

**Applies to:** UC06   **Scope:** case   **Source version:** 1

## Purpose
This procedure guides bank staff on reviewing card spend when transaction descriptors are unclear, so that any discussion of card suitability is based on verified merchant information, confirmed spending context and the customer’s stated tolerance for an annual fee.

## 1. Scope and principle

Apply this procedure when reviewing card spending for suitability where one or more transaction descriptors are unclear or routed through a payment gateway.
Use verified facts from customer-provided material and the customer’s own stated preferences. Do not rely on assumptions drawn from unclear descriptors or isolated transactions.

## 2. Verify unclear spend before discussion

Before discussing whether the current card remains suitable, confirm the underlying merchant for any unclear or gateway-based transaction descriptor.
Use supporting material such as a receipt to identify the actual merchant behind the descriptor.

- Check the actual merchant behind any gateway descriptor from a receipt.
- If receipts are needed to clarify descriptors, ask for the receipts that would resolve the descriptors.

## 3. Confirm whether travel spend is recurring

Where there is a large travel-related spend, establish whether it reflects a recurring pattern or a single occasion.
Do not treat one trip or one large travel transaction as evidence of an ongoing travel pattern.

- Check whether a large travel spend is recurring or a single occasion.

## 4. Confirm fee tolerance directly

Ask the customer explicitly about their tolerance for an annual fee before discussing suitability involving a fee-bearing card.
Do not assume that headline benefits justify a higher-fee card, and do not treat fee acceptance as implied unless the customer has stated it.

- Check the customer's tolerance for an annual fee, asked explicitly.

## 5. Limits, stop points and permitted outcomes

Stop the review if key facts remain unresolved. Do not continue to a suitability discussion where charges remain unidentified or the customer’s fee tolerance is unstated.
If the review can proceed on verified facts, the permitted outcomes are limited to maintaining the current position, showing that no change is worthwhile, or requesting documents needed to clarify the spend.

- Charges remain unidentified: stop the review.
- Fee tolerance is unstated: stop the review.
- Valid outcomes are to ask for the receipts that would resolve the descriptors, keep the current card, or show that no change is worthwhile.

## Required checks
- Verify the actual merchant behind any gateway descriptor from a receipt.
- Confirm whether a large travel spend is recurring or a single occasion.
- Ask the customer explicitly about their tolerance for an annual fee.

## Prohibited assumptions
- Do not assume a spending category from an opaque descriptor.
- Do not assume that one trip establishes a travel pattern.
- Do not assume that a higher-fee card is justified by headline benefits.

## Stop conditions
- Stop if charges remain unidentified.
- Stop if fee tolerance is unstated.

## Valid outcomes
- Ask for the receipts that would resolve the descriptors.
- Keep the current card.
- Show that no change is worthwhile.

## Human gate
Only a human staff member may authorise any final suitability wording shared with the customer after reviewing the verified facts and stated fee tolerance.
