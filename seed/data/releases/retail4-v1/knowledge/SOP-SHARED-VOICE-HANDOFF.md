# Voice support handoff (SOP-SHARED-VOICE-HANDOFF)

Synthetic Contoso demo procedure - fictional internal policy, not legal or regulatory authority.

**Applies to:** all cases   **Scope:** shared   **Source version:** 1

## Purpose
This procedure sets out what bank staff may and may not do during a voice support handoff, including the permissions that must be in place before any case context is shared and the limited outcomes that may follow from the call.

## Scope and policy basis

This is a Synthetic Contoso demo procedure - fictional internal policy, not legal or regulatory authority.
Apply this procedure whenever a voice call is used to continue, clarify or support an existing customer case.

## Permission requirements before the call

Obtain and verify separate permissions before proceeding with a voice support handoff.
Staff must not treat one form of permission as covering another activity.

- Confirm permission for audio processing separately from permission to share case context.
- Ensure the exact context to be shared is shown before the call starts.

## Call handling rules

During the call, limit discussion to the accepted scope and do not exceed the permissions given.
A spoken statement on a call is not, by itself, authority to carry out actions involving funds, cards or products.

- Only discuss case context that was explicitly presented before the call started.
- If the customer wishes to proceed beyond discussion, prepare the next step only through an appropriate confirmation path outside the call.

## Prohibited assumptions

Staff must not rely on implied consent or broad interpretations of what the customer may have meant.

## Stop and outcome handling

End or pause the handoff immediately when the required permission basis is not in place.
When the call proceeds validly, record only the permitted result.

- Valid outcomes are limited to discussing an accepted artifact or drafting a request for the customer to confirm on the webpage.

## Required checks
- Obtain separate permission for audio processing and for sharing case context.
- Show the exact context being shared before the call starts.

## Prohibited assumptions
- Do not assume that permission to process audio permits sharing documents.
- Do not assume that a spoken agreement authorises money, a card change or a product.

## Stop conditions
- Stop if either permission is absent or has been revoked.

## Valid outcomes
- Discuss an accepted artifact.
- Draft a request for the customer to confirm on the webpage.

## Human gate
Only a human staff member may authorise any action beyond discussion, including any step that could affect money, a card change or a product.
