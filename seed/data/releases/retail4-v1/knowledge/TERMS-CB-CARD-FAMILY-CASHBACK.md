## Who this suits
This card suits customers aged 21 or above with a monthly income of at least INR 50,000.00 who spend a meaningful share of their monthly budget on groceries, education and utilities. It can also suit those who want a card that can be used internationally.

## Benefits
- Earn 5.0% cashback on groceries, education and utilities.
- Cashback on the higher-rate categories is available up to a monthly cap of INR 500.00.
- Spends outside groceries, education and utilities still earn 0.5% cashback.
- The card can be used for international transactions.
- The annual fee is waived when yearly spending reaches INR 2,50,000.00.

## Limitations
- There is an annual fee of INR 999.00 if the spend waiver condition is not met.
- The 5.0% cashback is capped at INR 500.00 per month, so heavy spending in the bonus categories will stop earning at that higher rate beyond the cap.
- All spending outside groceries, education and utilities earns only 0.5% cashback.
- You need to be at least 21 years old to apply.
- Income evidence is required, and the minimum monthly income is INR 50,000.00.

## Fees
- Annual fee of INR 999.00.
- Annual fee waiver on yearly spend of INR 2,50,000.00.

## Who this does not suit
This may be a poor fit for customers whose spending is mostly outside groceries, education and utilities, because those transactions earn only 0.5% cashback. It may also not suit anyone unwilling to pay an annual fee of INR 999.00 unless they expect to reach yearly spending of INR 2,50,000.00.

## Main trade-off
Strong on household categories, but the 5% is capped monthly and everything outside those categories earns only 0.5% - so the higher fee is only recovered by a genuinely household-weighted spend pattern.

## Rate terms
You earn 5.0% cashback on groceries, education and utilities, subject to a monthly cap of INR 500.00. All other eligible spending earns 0.5% cashback.

## Access and liquidity
This is a credit card, so it is meant for spending rather than parking money for withdrawal. It can be used for domestic and international transactions.
