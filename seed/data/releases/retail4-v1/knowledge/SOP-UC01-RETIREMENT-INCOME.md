# Retirement income and deposit review (SOP-UC01-RETIREMENT-INCOME)

Synthetic Contoso demo procedure - fictional internal policy, not legal or regulatory authority.

**Applies to:** UC01   **Scope:** case   **Source version:** 1

## Purpose
This procedure sets out how Contoso staff should review a retiring customer’s deposit and income position using the customer’s stated needs and the documents provided, while avoiding assumptions and limiting the outcome to explanation or no-change handling where required.

## 1. Scope and intent

Apply this procedure when reviewing a retiring customer’s deposit and income position under UC01.
Use the customer’s stated priorities and the supplied deposit records to frame the review. Keep the discussion factual, balanced and limited to what is evidenced in the case material.

- Treat this as a synthetic Contoso demo procedure - fictional internal policy, not legal or regulatory authority.

## 2. Review approach

Begin by understanding what the customer has said matters more: regular income or access to funds. Do not proceed on guesswork if this is not stated.
Check each existing deposit carefully before discussing any possible next step. Confirm what the document says, not what may usually apply.

## 3. Assumptions that are not permitted

Staff must not infer customer preference from age, balance size or a potentially higher return. If a priority is not stated, it remains unstated until the customer clarifies it.

## 4. When to stop and seek clarification

Stop the review if a required customer preference or document detail is missing or unreadable. Record the gap and obtain clarification before continuing.

## 5. Permitted outcomes

The review may end only in a limited set of factual outcomes. Keep the discussion explanatory and avoid implying a preferred choice or approval.

## Required checks
- Review the customer’s own stated priority between regular income and access to funds.
- Check the exact maturity date and standing instruction on each existing deposit.
- Verify whether the renewal rate is stated or merely described as 'prevailing'.
- Look for recent irregular outflows that indicate a genuine need for liquidity.

## Prohibited assumptions
- Do not assume that a higher payout rate is preferable.
- Do not assume that a customer aged over 60 wants a senior product.
- Do not assume that an unstated priority can be inferred from age or balance.

## Stop conditions
- Stop if the customer has not stated a liquidity preference.
- Stop if the renewal terms cannot be read from the document supplied.

## Valid outcomes
- Explain the trade-off and ask which matters more.
- Keep the current deposit unchanged.
- No product change, analysis only.

## Human gate
Only a human staff member may authorise any customer-facing interpretation of ambiguous deposit terms or any action beyond analysis and explanation.
