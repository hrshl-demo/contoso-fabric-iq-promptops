## Purpose
This procedure guides bank staff on interpreting a group settlement conversation using available supporting records, participant statements and posted activity, while clearly separating provisional understanding from confirmed payment or transfer.

## 1. Scope and policy status

This is a Synthetic Contoso demo procedure - fictional internal policy, not legal or regulatory authority.
Apply this procedure when reviewing a conversation about shared expenses, reimbursements or informal group settlement between participants.

## 2. Core review approach

Assess the conversation conservatively and rely on supporting evidence for each item before treating it as part of a draft settlement.
Where details remain unclear, record the uncertainty plainly rather than filling gaps with assumptions.

- Use receipts, transaction records and participant statements together to form a working view.
- Treat mentions in chat as discussion points unless supported by evidence or clear participant confirmation.

Complete the following checks before preparing any draft split or interpretation.

## 4. Handling uncertainty and disputes

If any item is contested, preserve the disputed status in the record and do not present it as settled fact.
Do not convert a discussion into a completed payment outcome unless there is evidence of a posted transfer or other confirmed movement of funds.

- Flag contested expenses, attendance and reimbursements clearly.
- Keep disputed items separate from any working split until clarified.

## 5. Permitted case outcomes

Where the record is incomplete but usable, staff may document a provisional interpretation with appropriate flags.
Any final acceptance of disputed matters requires human judgement based on the available evidence.

- A draft split may be prepared only with disputed items clearly flagged.
- A saved agreement must not be treated as a transfer.

## Required checks
- Verify each expense against an actual receipt or transaction.
- Confirm who a participant says attended which part.
- Check whether a refund actually posted or was only mentioned.

## Prohibited assumptions
- Do not assume that a chat message is any participant's consent.
- Do not assume an equal split when participation differed.
- Do not assume that a disputed cash payment occurred.

## Stop conditions
- Stop if participation or payment is disputed and unresolved.

## Valid outcomes
- Produce a draft split with the disputed items flagged.
- A saved agreement is not a transfer.

## Human gate
Only a human may authorise acceptance of a disputed participation or payment position for case handling.
