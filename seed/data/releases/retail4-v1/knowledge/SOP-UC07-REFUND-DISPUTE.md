# Purchase and refund resolution (SOP-UC07-REFUND-DISPUTE)

Synthetic Contoso demo procedure - fictional internal policy, not legal or regulatory authority.

**Applies to:** UC07   **Scope:** case   **Source version:** 1

## Purpose
This procedure guides bank staff on handling a case where a merchant has said a refund will be processed but no refund may yet have been received. The aim is to confirm the underlying purchase, verify whether any credit has actually posted, and communicate the next steps and evidence timeline clearly without selling any product or service.

## 1. Scope and policy status

This is a Synthetic Contoso demo procedure - fictional internal policy, not legal or regulatory authority.
Use this procedure for cases where a customer reports that a merchant has promised a refund but the refund may not have reached the account.

- Do not treat a merchant statement as proof that funds have moved.
- Selling is prohibited in this interaction.

## 2. Core verification steps

Start with the purchase record and the account ledger. Confirm the transaction before discussing any refund status.
Use only posted transaction evidence and the merchant's stated timeline when explaining the case status.

- Check whether any matching credit exists in the ledger; a promise is not a posting.

## 3. Evidence handling

Record what the customer or merchant has shared, but separate statements from posted ledger facts.
Where a merchant message refers to a refund, capture it as supporting context only unless a matching credit is visible in the ledger.

- Rely on the purchase record for the amount under discussion.
- Note the stated refund window exactly as provided by the merchant, if available.

## 4. Prohibited assumptions

Staff must not fill gaps with assumptions when evidence is incomplete or still pending.

- Do not assume a refund amount from a chat instead of the purchase record.

## 5. Stop and outcome handling

If the purchase cannot be matched, do not proceed as though the refund request has been verified.
Where the purchase is identified but no credit is visible, communicate the present position plainly and set expectations on evidence and timing.

- Stop if no matching purchase can be identified.
- Valid outcomes are to register the request and set out the evidence timeline.
- Valid outcomes are to state plainly that no credit has been received.

## Required checks
- Verify the original purchase transaction and its exact amount.
- Check whether any matching credit exists in the ledger; do not treat a promise as a posting.
- Confirm the merchant's stated refund window and whether it has started.

## Prohibited assumptions
- Do not assume that a merchant's assurance means money has moved.
- Do not infer a refund amount from a chat rather than the purchase record.

## Stop conditions
- Stop the review if no matching purchase can be identified.

## Valid outcomes
- Register the request and set out the evidence timeline.
- State plainly that no credit has been received.

## Human gate
Only a human staff member may authorise any final case note that concludes the present status of the refund request.
