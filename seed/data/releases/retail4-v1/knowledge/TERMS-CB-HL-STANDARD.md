## Who this suits
This can suit salaried or self-employed customers aged 23 or above who are buying a home and want a floating-rate home loan with a tenure of up to 25 years. It is more relevant for borrowers who can provide income evidence and property documents and who do not expect to prepay early.

## Benefits
- Floating interest rate at 8.45%, which may suit customers comfortable with a rate that can move over time.
- Loan funding is available up to 80% of the property value, which can reduce the upfront amount needed from your own funds.
- Repayment tenure can go up to 25 years, which can help spread the cost over a longer period.
- Processing fee is 0.5% of the loan amount, stated clearly at the start.
- The product is open to applicants from age 23, with maturity allowed up to age 65.

## Limitations
- Prepayment is locked for the first 12 months.
- After the lock-in period, prepayment charge is 2.0%.
- The main trade-off is the lower headline rate, but prepayment is locked for twelve months and then charged. Costly if a bonus is meant to shorten the loan.
- You must provide income evidence as part of the application.
- You must provide property documents, and the loan is subject to underwriting.

## Fees
- Processing fee of 0.5% of the loan amount.
- Prepayment charge of 2.0% after the first 12 months, with prepayment locked during the first 12 months.

## Who this does not suit
This may be a poor fit for customers who expect to use a bonus or lump sum to close the loan early, because prepayment is locked for 12 months and then charged. It is also not suitable for anyone who cannot provide income evidence or property documents.

## Main trade-off
The lower headline rate, but prepayment is locked for twelve months and then charged. Costly if a bonus is meant to shorten the loan.

## Rate terms
Interest is charged at a floating rate of 8.45%, so the rate can change over the life of the loan.

## Access and liquidity
This is a home loan, not a deposit product. Money is not available for withdrawal like savings; if you want to reduce the loan early, prepayment is not allowed for the first 12 months and is charged at 2.0% after that.
