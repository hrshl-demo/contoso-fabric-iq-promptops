## Who this suits
This may suit adults who already have a base health policy or other cover for the deductible and want extra protection above that threshold. It can also fit someone looking for ₹10,00,000 top-up cover with a monthly premium of ₹1,200, while accepting waiting periods and shared claim costs.

## Benefits
- Provides ₹10,00,000 of health top-up cover.
- Monthly premium is ₹1,200.
- Entry age starts from 18 and goes up to 65.
- There is no room rent sub-limit on this cover.
- Designed to work above a deductible of ₹5,00,000 for people who already have base policy or deductible cover.

## Limitations
- Nothing is paid until the deductible of ₹5,00,000 is crossed.
- After the deductible is crossed, a 10% co-pay applies.
- There is an initial waiting period of 30 days.
- Pre-existing conditions have a waiting period of 24 months.
- A health declaration is required and underwriting is required.

## Fees
- Monthly premium is ₹1,200.

## Who this does not suit
This is a poor fit for someone who does not already have a base policy or other cover for the deductible, or who wants claims support from the first rupee of hospitalisation. It may also not suit anyone uncomfortable with a 30-day initial waiting period, a 24-month pre-existing condition waiting period, or paying 10% of eligible costs after the deductible.

## Main trade-off
Large cover for a small premium, but nothing is paid until the deductible is crossed, and a 10% co-pay applies after that.
