# Document review and fact adoption (SOP-SHARED-DOCUMENT-REVIEW)

Synthetic Contoso demo procedure - fictional internal policy, not legal or regulatory authority.

**Applies to:** all cases   **Scope:** shared   **Source version:** 1

## Purpose
This procedure sets out how bank staff must review extracted information from a document and determine when it may be treated as a usable fact for internal processing. The aim is to ensure that extracted values are traceable, customer-confirmed where required, and never treated as final merely because they appeared in a document.

## 1. Scope and principle

This procedure applies when information has been extracted from a document and is being reviewed for possible adoption as a usable fact.
Staff must treat extracted information as review material until the required checks are completed and an allowed outcome is recorded.

## 2. Review requirements

Before adopting any extracted value, complete the mandatory verification steps and record the basis for the decision.
Keep the review focused on the extracted value, its source location, and the customer's confirmation where applicable.

- Record the page and region from which each extracted value was taken.
- Ensure extraction confidence is reported separately from any other confidence measure.

## 3. Use of document content

A completed extraction must not be treated as an accepted fact by itself.
Information shown in a document must not be treated as overriding the bank's own records without proper review and handling.

## 4. Stop condition and escalation

Stop the review if a value is uncertain and the customer has not confirmed it.
Do not adopt that value as a usable fact until the issue is resolved through an allowed outcome.

## 5. Allowed outcomes

At the end of the review, record only one or more permitted outcomes that accurately reflect the result of the review.

## Required checks
- Confirm that the customer has reviewed and accepted each extracted value.
- Record the page and region an extracted value came from.
- Report extraction confidence separately from any other confidence.

## Prohibited assumptions
- Do not assume that a completed extraction is an accepted fact.
- Do not assume that a document's claim overrides the bank's own records.

## Stop conditions
- Stop if a value is uncertain and the customer has not confirmed it.

## Valid outcomes
- accept
- correct
- reject
- ask for a clearer document

## Human gate
Only a human staff member may authorise final adoption of a reviewed extracted value as a usable fact.
