## Who this suits
This can suit adults who need a prepaid card for international spending and already have a linked Contoso savings account. It may also suit someone who wants access without submitting income proof and prefers loading a set amount instead of borrowing.

## Benefits
- Foreign-currency transactions use a forex markup of 0.99%, which is the lowest in this range.
- There is no annual fee, so there is no yearly holding charge for the card.
- Income evidence is not required, which can make it reachable without a salary history.
- It can be used internationally.
- You can load up to INR 2,00,000.00 on the card.

## Limitations
- You must be at least 18 to apply.
- A linked Contoso savings account is required.
- There is no borrowing on this card, so you can only spend what has been loaded.
- There are no rewards or cashback on spending.
- There are no free ATM withdrawals abroad, and any balance left on the card has to be refunded back rather than simply remaining in an account.

## Fees
- Issuance fee: INR 199.00.
- Annual fee: 0.
- Reload fee: INR 100.00 per load.
- Refund fee: INR 250.00.
- Free ATM withdrawals abroad: 0.

## Who this does not suit
This is a poor fit for someone who wants credit, rewards, or free ATM cash withdrawals abroad. It may also not suit a customer who expects to reload often or keep leftover funds parked, because reloads and the final refund both carry flat fees.

## Main trade-off
The lowest foreign-exchange markup in the range, no borrowing and no income evidence, so it is reachable without a salary history - but every load and the final refund carry a flat fee, there are no rewards, no free ATM withdrawals abroad, and money left on the card has to be refunded back rather than simply staying in the account.

## Rate terms
Foreign-currency transactions carry a forex markup of 0.99%. There is no cashback or rewards rate on spending.

## Access and liquidity
This is a prepaid card for loaded funds, with international use. Money left on the card has to be refunded back, and the refund carries a fee of INR 250.00.
