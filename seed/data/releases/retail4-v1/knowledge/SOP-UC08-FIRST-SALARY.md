# First salary account review (SOP-UC08-FIRST-SALARY)

**Applies to:** UC08   **Scope:** case   **Source version:** 1

## Purpose
This procedure guides bank staff on how to assist a customer who is planning before the first salary credit arrives, using only posted income facts and the fixed monthly component for any salary-linked review.

## 1. Scope and policy note

This is a Synthetic Contoso demo procedure - fictional internal policy, not legal or regulatory authority.
Use this procedure when a customer asks about salary-linked account handling or related planning before the first salary has been credited.

## 2. Core review approach

Base the discussion on verified income structure and actual account activity. Treat planning before first salary as preliminary only until salary credit is visible in the account.
Keep the conversation factual and limited to timing, posted salary status, and fixed monthly income relevant to eligibility checks.

- Separate the fixed monthly component from any variable, annual, or pro-rated element before discussing salary-linked handling.
- Confirm whether a salary credit has actually posted; an offer is not income.
- Check the eligibility threshold of any salary product against the fixed amount only.

## 3. Handling before first salary credit

If the customer is awaiting the first salary, explain that salary-linked activation depends on an actual posted salary credit. Do not treat expected earnings as already available income.
If the income structure includes annual or variable components, keep these distinct from the fixed monthly amount during the review.

## 4. Decision boundaries

Do not proceed with activation of a salary product where no salary has posted.
Where the customer asks about bundles linked to salary status before income posts, keep the response neutral and within the valid outcomes of this procedure.

## 5. Documentation and escalation

Record the basis of the discussion clearly, including whether salary has posted and what fixed monthly component was identified for review.
Any exception, override, or activation despite unclear or absent posted salary must be referred for human approval.

## Required checks
- Identify the fixed monthly component separately from any variable or annual element.
- Verify whether a salary credit has actually posted; do not treat an offer as income.
- Assess the eligibility threshold of any salary product against the fixed amount only.

## Prohibited assumptions
- Do not assume that a headline annual figure is monthly cash.
- Do not assume that a variable or pro-rated bonus contributes to early months.

## Stop conditions
- Stop the process if no salary has posted, because a salary product cannot be activated.

## Valid outcomes
- Explain the timing.
- Prepare nothing until income posts.
- Decline bundles.

## Human gate
Only a human may authorise any exception, override, or activation when posted salary status is absent or unclear.
