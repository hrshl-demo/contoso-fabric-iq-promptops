## Who this suits
This may suit a home buyer who wants flexibility to prepay at any time and may make use of an overdraft facility during the loan term. It is more relevant for applicants aged 23 or above whose loan can finish by age 65 and who can provide income and property documents.

## Benefits
- Floating interest rate of 8.75%, so the rate can move over time rather than staying fixed for the full tenure.
- Unlimited free prepayment, because the prepayment charge is 0.0% and the lock-in period is 0 months.
- Overdraft facility is available, which can add flexibility in how funds are accessed and managed.
- Maximum tenure of 20 years can help spread repayments over a longer period.
- Financing is available up to 75% of the property value, which sets the maximum loan-to-value for this product.

## Limitations
- The main trade-off is a higher rate and a lower maximum loan-to-value, with the flexibility benefits making more sense only if prepayment actually happens.
- The interest rate is floating, so repayments may change over time.
- The maximum loan-to-value is 75%, so you will need to arrange the remaining property cost from your own sources.
- Eligibility is restricted: minimum age is 23 and the loan must mature by age 65.
- Income evidence and property documents are required, and underwriting is required before the loan can proceed.

## Fees
- Processing fee of 0.25% of the loan amount.
- Prepayment charge is 0.0%.

## Who this does not suit
This is a poor fit for someone who wants payment certainty from a fixed rate, needs to borrow more than 75% of the property value, or does not expect to prepay at all. It may also not suit applicants who cannot provide income evidence and property documents or whose loan term would run beyond age 65.

## Main trade-off
Higher rate and a lower maximum loan-to-value, but unlimited free prepayment and an overdraft facility. Better only if prepayment actually happens.
