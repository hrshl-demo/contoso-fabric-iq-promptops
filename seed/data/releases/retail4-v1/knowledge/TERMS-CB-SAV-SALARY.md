## Who this suits
This account suits salaried adults who receive at least INR 25,000.00 as verified salary credit each month and want a savings account without a minimum balance requirement. It is meant for customers who expect regular salary credits to continue.

## Benefits
- No minimum balance is required to keep the account.
- There is no monthly fee.
- Savings balances earn interest at 3.25%.
- Available to adults from age 18 onwards.
- Designed for customers with verified monthly salary credit of at least INR 25,000.00.

## Limitations
- You need verified salary credit of at least INR 25,000.00 each month to be eligible.
- If salary credit stops for two consecutive months, the account lapses to Classic terms.
- This is a savings account, so the main value depends on continuing salary credits rather than one-time funding.
- Customers below 18 are not eligible.
