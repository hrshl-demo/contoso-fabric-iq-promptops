# Home loan clause comparison (SOP-UC05-HOME-LOAN-CLAUSES)

Synthetic Contoso demo procedure - fictional internal policy, not legal or regulatory authority.

**Applies to:** UC05   **Scope:** case   **Source version:** 1

## Purpose
This procedure guides bank staff on comparing two indicative home loan structures in a clear, clause-by-clause manner against the customer’s stated intention, without implying approval or treating indicative items as fixed commitments.

## 1. Scope and status

This is a Synthetic Contoso demo procedure - fictional internal policy, not legal or regulatory authority.
Use this procedure when staff are asked to compare two indicative home loan structures for the same customer discussion.

- Treat both structures as indicative unless formally documented otherwise.
- Keep the discussion factual, limited to the supplied clauses and the customer’s stated intention.

## 2. Core comparison approach

Carry out a side-by-side comparison of the two structures only on the basis of the stated terms and the customer’s stated intention.
Explain each clause in plain Indian English and avoid presenting any option as preferred or approved.

- Compare the maximum loan-to-value, as this changes the required down payment.
- Check whether any expected bonus is contractual or only indicative.

## 3. Clarifications staff must give

Make clear that a lower headline rate does not by itself establish that one structure is cheaper overall.
Where a bonus is mentioned, state clearly whether it is contractual or merely indicative and do not treat an indicative bonus as income.
State clearly that neither structure is an approval and that no offer has been sanctioned.

- Explain the effect of loan-to-value on the customer’s required contribution.
- Explain how prepayment lock-in and prepayment charges matter if the customer intends to prepay.

## 4. Stop and defer rules

Do not complete the comparison against suitability of clauses if the customer’s prepayment intention is not known.
If that intention is unclear, pause the comparison and defer further clause-based discussion until it is known.

- Stop when the customer's prepayment intention is unknown.
- Record that the comparison has been deferred pending clarification of intention.

## 5. Permitted outcomes

The outcome of this process must remain limited to factual comparison and clarification.
Do not convert the comparison into a recommendation, ranking or approval statement.

## Required checks
- Compare the rate, processing fee, prepayment lock-in and prepayment charge in each offer.
- Verify the maximum loan-to-value, because it changes the required down payment.
- Confirm whether an expected bonus is contractual or indicative.

## Prohibited assumptions
- Do not assume that the lower headline rate is cheaper overall.
- Do not assume that an indicative bonus is income.
- Do not assume that any offer has been sanctioned, because none has.

## Stop conditions
- Stop if the customer's prepayment intention is unknown.

## Valid outcomes
- Compare clause by clause against the stated intention.
- Explain that neither is an approval.
- Defer until the intention is known.

## Human gate
Only a human staff member may authorise any final customer-facing interpretation where the customer’s intention is unclear or additional context is required.
