# Safe use of the product catalogue (SOP-SHARED-CATALOGUE-USE)

Synthetic Contoso demo procedure - fictional internal policy, not legal or regulatory authority.

**Applies to:** all cases   **Scope:** shared   **Source version:** 1

## Purpose
This procedure sets out how bank staff must source and quote catalogue facts so that any term shared internally or with a customer-facing team is taken only from permitted, current and approved catalogue data.

## Scope and intent

This synthetic Contoso demo procedure applies whenever staff need to refer to product catalogue facts, including terms, rates, fees, limits and required clauses.
Use this procedure to ensure catalogue information is quoted accurately, consistently and only from approved sources.

## Source of truth

Staff must use only the retrieved permitted set for the relevant product and version. Do not rely on memory, older material, similar products or informal references.
All rates, fees and limits must be taken directly from approved structured fields.

- Confirm that the product and version appear in the retrieved permitted set before quoting any term.
- Read every rate, fee and limit from approved structured fields only.

## Quoting catalogue facts

Quote only the approved term exactly as available in the catalogue material. If a required clause is not available or appears out of date, do not fill the gap from another source.
Where information is incomplete, state the gap plainly and seek clarification through the appropriate internal route.

- Use the approved term when it is available and current.
- If information cannot be confirmed, withhold and ask.
- If a gap remains, state the gap plainly.

## Prohibited assumptions

Staff must not bridge missing catalogue information by assumption. If a fact is not present in the approved catalogue content, it must not be introduced.

## Stop and escalate

Stop the quoting process immediately if a required clause is missing or stale. No workaround is permitted in such cases.
Escalate for review through the designated human owner so that the missing or stale content can be resolved before any term is quoted further.

## Required checks
- Confirm that the product and version are in the retrieved permitted set.
- Read every rate, fee and limit from approved structured fields.

## Prohibited assumptions
- Do not assume a term that is not in the catalogue.
- Do not assume that a similar product's terms apply.

## Stop conditions
- Stop if a required clause is missing or stale.

## Valid outcomes
- Quote the approved term.
- Withhold and ask.
- State the gap plainly.

## Human gate
Only a human may authorise any resolution of missing, stale or unclear catalogue content before further use.
