## Who this suits
This suits adults who want to place at least INR 10,000.00 for a short period without locking it away. It can fit someone keeping money aside for a need that may arise within 3 to 12 months.

## Benefits
- Minimum entry amount is INR 10,000.00, so the starting amount is relatively small for a fixed deposit.
- You can choose a term from 3 months up to 12 months, which suits short-duration parking of funds.
- Partial withdrawal is allowed from the start, with no waiting period.
- There is no penalty for partial withdrawal, which gives flexibility if you need some of the money back.
- The deposit can be broken before maturity with no penalty, so the full amount is not trapped if plans change.

## Limitations
- Interest is 6.1%, and the main trade-off is that this is the lowest deposit rate in the range.
- The deposit cannot run beyond 12 months, so it is not meant for longer-term parking.
- Interest is paid on maturity, so there is no periodic payout during the term.
- You must be at least 18 to open it.
- You need a minimum principal of INR 10,000.00 to start.

## Fees
- No fees apply to this product.

## Who this does not suit
This is a poor fit for someone who wants a higher deposit rate and is comfortable locking money in for longer. It is also not suitable for anyone who needs a term beyond 12 months or wants interest paid out before maturity.

## Main trade-off
Can be broken at any time at no penalty and needs only a small principal, so money that may be needed in a few months is not trapped - but it pays the lowest deposit rate in the range and cannot run beyond twelve months. The rate is what the freedom costs.

## Rate terms
Interest is 6.1%, and it is paid when the deposit matures.

## Access and liquidity
Money can be accessed from the start through partial withdrawal, with no penalty for partial withdrawal. The deposit can also be closed before maturity with no penalty.
