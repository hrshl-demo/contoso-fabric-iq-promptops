# Medical estimate and cover review (SOP-UC02-MEDICAL-COVER)

Synthetic Contoso demo procedure - fictional internal policy, not legal or regulatory authority.

**Applies to:** UC02   **Scope:** case   **Source version:** 1

## Purpose
This procedure guides bank staff on helping a customer understand a medical estimate against an existing policy by checking the policy wording, identifying any unclear points, and limiting the discussion to what is supported by the document and the customer’s stated consent.

## 1. Scope and intent

This is a Synthetic Contoso demo procedure - fictional internal policy, not legal or regulatory authority.
Use this procedure when a customer asks for help to understand an estimate against an existing policy.
Keep the discussion factual, document-based and limited to the policy wording and the estimate presented.

## 2. Review approach

Read the policy terms and the estimate together before commenting on applicability.
Explain the policy wording in plain Indian English without extending beyond what the document states.
Where a point is uncertain, say clearly that it is unclear and why it cannot yet be confirmed.

## 3. Consent and discussion boundaries

Do not begin any product discussion unless the customer has explicitly opted in.
If the customer does not opt in, keep the interaction strictly to explaining the existing policy wording and the estimate.
A customer’s distress, urgency or concern must not be treated as consent to discuss any product.

## 4. Prohibited assumptions

Staff must not fill gaps in the documents by assumption.
Only state what is written in the policy and estimate, and identify any missing information plainly.

## 5. Stop and escalate

Stop the applicability review when the document does not support a reliable view.
Tell the customer exactly what information is missing or what consent has not been provided, and limit the conversation accordingly.

## 6. Permitted outcomes

Close the interaction with a clear factual summary of what was checked and what remains uncertain.
Where needed, help the customer frame a precise follow-up with the insurer without adding assumptions.

## Required checks
- Check the policy’s stated sub-limits, waiting periods and pre-hospitalisation window.
- Check whether each estimate line item carries a date that places it inside that window.
- Confirm that the customer has explicitly opted in before any product is discussed.

## Prohibited assumptions
- Do not assume that an expense is covered because it appears medical.
- Do not assume any health condition beyond what the document states.
- Do not assume that distress implies consent to discuss a product.

## Stop conditions
- Stop if a line item’s date is absent, so applicability cannot be determined.
- Stop if the customer has not opted in to product discussion.

## Valid outcomes
- Explain what the policy says and identify exactly what is unclear.
- Help the customer ask the insurer a specific question.
- No cover discussion at all if the customer declines.

## Human gate
Only a human staff member may authorise any product discussion after confirming the customer’s explicit opt-in.
