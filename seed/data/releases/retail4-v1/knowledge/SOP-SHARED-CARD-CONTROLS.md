## Purpose
This procedure sets out how bank staff must handle a request to change a card control, ensuring the specific control, its present setting, and the customer’s explicit confirmation on the webpage are reviewed before any instruction is recorded.

## 1. Scope and policy status

This is a Synthetic Contoso demo procedure - fictional internal policy, not legal or regulatory authority.
Use this procedure when reviewing a request relating to a change in card controls.

## 2. Review the requested change

Staff must identify precisely what card control is being changed before proceeding.
Staff must review the current state of that same control so that the requested change is understood in context.

- Confirm the exact control being changed.
- Confirm the current state of that control.

## 3. Confirmation requirement

Do not treat a spoken request as confirmation.
Proceed only where there is explicit human confirmation on the webpage for the reviewed instruction.

- Check for explicit human confirmation on the webpage.
- If the webpage does not show confirmation, do not record the instruction.

## 4. When to stop

Stop the review immediately if confirmation has not been given in the reviewed form.

## 5. Permitted outcomes

Once all required checks are complete, close the review using only a valid outcome under this procedure.

## Required checks
- Confirm the exact control being changed and its current state.
- Obtain explicit human confirmation on the webpage.

## Prohibited assumptions
- Do not assume that a spoken request is a confirmation.

## Stop conditions
- Stop if confirmation has not been given in the reviewed form.

## Valid outcomes
- Record an acknowledged instruction.
- Cancel.

## Human gate
Only a human may authorise the recording of the acknowledged instruction after verifying the webpage confirmation.
