This can suit an adult customer who keeps higher balances in a linked savings account and wants surplus funds to move automatically while still being able to access money quickly. It is more relevant for someone comfortable with a sweep structure rather than a simple plain savings balance.

- Balances can earn 6.5% interest on the swept portion.
- There is no monthly fee.
- Money can come back through instant reverse sweep when needed.
- Amounts above INR 1,00,000.00 can be considered for sweeping instead of remaining fully in the savings account.
- Sweeping happens in fixed multiples of INR 25,000.00, which can help keep the movement rule clear and predictable.

- Sweeping happens only in multiples of INR 25,000.00, so some excess balance may remain unswept.
- The main trade-off is: Deposit-like interest with instant reverse sweep, but only balances above the threshold sweep, and only in fixed multiples - so a portion always earns the savings rate.
