## Eligibility conditions

## Who this suits
This suits an adult customer who already has a linked Contoso savings account and usually keeps a small or uneven balance. It can be useful for someone who wants extra funds above a basic level to move automatically without needing a large balance.

## Benefits
- Available to customers aged 18 and above who have a linked savings account.
- No monthly fee is charged.
- Sweeping starts once the balance goes above INR 10,000.00, so it can begin working at a relatively low balance.
- Amounts are swept in multiples of INR 1,000.00, which can suit smaller or uneven balances.
- Reverse sweep is instant, so money can come back quickly when needed.

## Limitations
- It pays 5.5% interest, which is lower than the standard sweep mentioned in the trade-off.
- Sweeping stops above the ceiling of INR 1,00,000.00, so it may not suit customers whose balance regularly grows beyond that level.
- You must have a linked savings account to use it.
- Only customers aged 18 or above are eligible.
- The main trade-off is that it works on a small or uneven balance, but it is likely to be outgrown rather than kept.

## Fees

## Who this does not suit
This is a poor fit for someone who wants a higher sweep return or expects to hold balances above INR 1,00,000.00 for long periods. It is also not suitable for anyone who does not already have a linked savings account or is below 18 years of age.

## Main trade-off
Starts sweeping at a far lower balance and in much smaller multiples, so it works on a small or uneven balance - but it pays less than the standard sweep and stops sweeping above its ceiling, so it is outgrown rather than kept.

## Rate terms
Interest is 5.5% on the swept amount.

## Access and liquidity
Money can be accessed through instant reverse sweep, with sweeping starting above INR 10,000.00, moving funds in multiples of INR 1,000.00, and stopping above the ceiling of INR 1,00,000.00.
