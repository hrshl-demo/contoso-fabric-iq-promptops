# Competing household commitments (SOP-UC04-FAMILY-DECISIONS)

Synthetic Contoso demo procedure - fictional internal policy, not legal or regulatory authority.

**Applies to:** UC04   **Scope:** case   **Source version:** 1

## Purpose
This procedure guides staff on handling discussions where multiple household commitments may draw on the same surplus, so that facts are confirmed from source documents, uncertain items are kept separate from decided commitments, and the customer’s own priorities are recorded before any trade-off is discussed.

## 1. Scope and policy status

This is a Synthetic Contoso demo procedure - fictional internal policy, not legal or regulatory authority.
Use this procedure when several household commitments may compete for the same available surplus in the same discussion.

## 2. Core handling approach

Keep the conversation factual, neutral and customer-led.
Separate confirmed commitments from possible commitments before discussing any trade-off.

- Do not choose on the customer’s behalf.
- Do not present any option as preferred.
- Do not imply that a possible commitment is already decided.

## 3. Verification steps

Before discussing competing uses of surplus, verify the underlying facts from the relevant source material and confirm the customer’s stated priorities explicitly.

- Check each commitment's actual amount and timing from its source document.
- Ask for the customer's own ranking explicitly and record it clearly.

## 4. Discussion boundaries

Where commitments compete, staff may explain the trade-off only after the customer’s ranking has been stated.
Any explanation must remain neutral and must not turn into a choice made by staff.

- If ranking is not available, pause the comparison and ask for it first.
- If no further action is suitable, conclude with no product.

## 5. Escalation and closure

Stop the process if the customer has not ranked the competing commitments.
Resume only when the customer has provided their own order of priority.

## Required checks
- Verify each commitment's actual amount and timing from its source document.
- Confirm which commitments are decided and which are only possible.
- Ask for the customer's own ranking explicitly.

## Prohibited assumptions
- Do not assume that a possible relocation is a decision.
- Do not assume a ranking the customer has not given.

## Stop conditions
- Stop if the customer has not ranked competing commitments.

## Valid outcomes
- Ask for a ranking.
- Show the trade-off without choosing.
- No product.

## Human gate
Only a human staff member may authorise any final interpretation of the customer’s stated priorities in the case record.
