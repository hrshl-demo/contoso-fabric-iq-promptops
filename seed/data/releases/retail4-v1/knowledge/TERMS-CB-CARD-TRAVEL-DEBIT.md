## Who this suits
This card suits adults who already keep a savings account with Contoso and want to use their own money for international spending. It can fit travellers who want overseas card use with a lower foreign-exchange markup than the credit cards, while avoiding borrowing.

## Benefits
- It can be used internationally for spending outside India.
- The foreign-exchange markup is 1.5%, which is lower than the credit cards mentioned in the trade-off.
- You get 2 free ATM withdrawals abroad.
- Because it is a debit card, spending is limited to money already held rather than borrowed.
- It is available to customers aged 18 and above who have a linked savings account.

## Limitations
- There is an annual fee of INR 499.00.
- You must already have a linked Contoso savings account to use this product.
- It is only for customers aged 18 or above.
- It spends only money already held, so it does not provide borrowing.
- The main trade-off is: Lower foreign-exchange markup than the credit cards and no borrowing, but it carries a fee and spends only money already held.
