## Who this suits
This may suit home buyers who want a fixed monthly home loan instalment and prefer repayment certainty over possible savings if market rates fall. It is meant for customers aged 23 or above who can provide income proof and property documents.

## Benefits
- The interest rate is fixed at 9.25%, so the instalment stays the same through the loan term.
- You can take the loan for up to 20 years, which can help spread repayment over a longer period.
- The product allows borrowing up to 75% of the property value, with the remaining amount to be arranged by the customer.
- The age criteria are clearly defined: minimum age is 23, and the loan must finish by age 65.
- The processing fee is 0.5%, giving a clearly stated upfront charge for handling the application.

## Limitations
- The main trade-off is that the instalment is fixed for the whole term, so the repayment never moves whatever happens to rates - but it is the highest rate in the range, prepayment is locked for twenty-four months and then charged, and the maximum loan-to-value is lower. Paying more from the start is what buys the certainty, and that is a loss if rates fall.
- The maximum loan-to-value is 75%, so you will need to bring in a larger own contribution than with a higher-LTV loan.
- Prepayment is locked for the first 24 months.
- After that lock-in period, prepayment is charged at 2.0%.
- This product requires underwriting, so the application is not just a formality and supporting documents are needed.

## Fees
- Processing fee of 0.5%.
- Prepayment charge of 2.0% after the first 24 months.

## Who this does not suit
This is a poor fit for someone who wants flexibility to prepay early or who is trying to maximise borrowing against the property value. It may also not suit a customer who expects falling rates and would prefer repayments that could reduce instead of staying fixed.

## Rate terms
Interest is charged at a fixed rate of 9.25% for the loan, so the repayment amount does not change during the term.

## Access and liquidity
This is a home loan, so money is not available like a deposit balance. Prepayment is locked for the first 24 months, and after that any prepayment attracts a charge of 2.0%.
